Innovative Security Solutions for Modern Access Control
#

Chiyu Technology offers a diverse portfolio of access control products, combining advanced hardware and robust software to address the evolving needs of security and facility management. Our solutions are tailored for flexibility, reliability, and integration across various industries and applications.

Hardware Solutions
#

Our hardware lineup encompasses a variety of access control devices, each engineered for specific security scenarios:

  • Biometric Controllers: Enhance security with fingerprint and facial recognition systems, including:

    • Biosense
    • BioSense II
    • BioSense III
    • BioSenseIII-T
    • CSS-V15Q
    • CSS-V25Q

  • Door Control Panels: Centralized management of entry points with models such as:

    • SEMAC CP102
    • SEMAC CP202
    • SEMAC D

  • Lift Controllers: Secure and manage elevator access with options like:

    • SEMAC-EP
    • SEMAC S3-V3
    • SEMAC S3-V4

  • RFID Security Door Access Controllers: Reliable proximity solutions, including:

    • CSS-800
    • CSS-R11
    • CSS-R16
    • CSS-R21
    • Webpass

  • Wiegand/OSDP Readers: Secure communication and compatibility with:

    • WR-M5 & TWR-M5
    • WR-M7/TWR-M7
    • WR-M7S
    • WR-QM

  • Serial Converters and Digital IO Controllers: Seamless integration and control with devices such as:

    • BF-430
    • CYT-100SC
    • CYT-133SC
    • BF-1010

  • Access Control Accessories: Enhance system functionality with:

    • WR-M7-BU USB card reader/writer
    • Mifare DESFire Programmer
    • BF-50
    • CSS-A11
    • CSS-A16
    • CSS-A15
    • BF-333
    • Magnetic Lock and Exit Button
    • MR-303U MIFARE CARD READER/WRITER (ENCODE/DECODE)
    • EM-302/ MF-302 USB card reader
